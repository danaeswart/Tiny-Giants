Who we are

Tiny Giants is a storybook-and-games world for small kids, with a calmer control room for the grown-ups looking after them. One brand, two voices: loud and playful for children, clear and quiet for adults.

Playful

Chunky shapes, bright colour, things that squash when you press them.

Safe

Big targets, no tricks, nothing scary. Kids can never get lost.

Warm

Cream paper, plum ink, friendly words. Never cold or corporate.

Voice in one lineTalk to kids like a kind storyteller. Talk to adults like a calm friend.

Hard rules
  • 16 px is the floor. Nothing renders smaller, even if you ask.
  • Line height is 1.3 times the size, unless it's a big title.
  • Respect system text size, capped at 1.4x so landscape screens don't overflow.
  • Left-align paragraphs. Centre only short labels and buttons.
  • Text is Night plum on light, white on blue or plum.

Touch and access

  • 48 px minimum for grown-ups

Tiny Giants style guide11
Quick checks

Do and don't

Do
  • Keep Night plum for text, never pure black
  • Use one primary button per screen
  • Give every colour a soft tint partner for backgrounds
  • Use Talina large, Poppins small
Don't
  • Put electric blue or DM Mono on the child side
  • Use text below 16 px or targets below 48 px
  • Put white boxes on the cream background (grown-up)
  • Use more than three bright colours in one view
Stay tiny.
Think giant.
